How to play

The goal: use every tile in one grid of real words, joined up like a crossword. Across words read left to right, down words top to bottom.

  1. Aim: tap a square. Tap it again to turn between → across and ↓ down.
  2. Spell: tap tiles in your hand, or type on a keyboard. Each tile lands at the cursor, which moves along the arrow and hops over tiles already there. Or type a whole word in the box and press Place.
  3. Peel: when your hand is empty and every word checks out, Peel to draw one more tile.
  4. Dump: stuck with a hard letter? Give one tile back and take three. Every draw comes from the full set of 144 tiles, so a dumped letter rarely comes back; 40 or 72 is just how many tiles you play.
  5. Bananas! When the bunch is empty and so is your hand, call Bananas! to stop the clock.

Stuck? Hint finds a word that fits, with its meaning and an example sentence. Tap any word on your board, then What’s …?, to look it up.

Fixing things: ⌫ takes back the last tile. Tap a tile on the board, then Take back, to lift a whole word. Undo steps back. Red tiles are in a word the dictionary doesn’t know; a dashed outline means that piece isn’t joined to the rest.

Moving around: drag to scroll, pinch or use + and − to zoom, and ⌖ to fit the grid on screen.

Keyboard: letters type, arrows move (or turn), Space turns, Backspace takes back, Enter peels, Ctrl+Z undoes.

Words: the ENABLE list (public domain, 172,820 words), plus qi, za, ok and ew, and any you add under My words. Meanings and examples: WordNet 3.0, copyright 2006 by Princeton University (licence at the top of meanings.txt).
