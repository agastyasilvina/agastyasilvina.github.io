How the review works

Each kanji gives two cards: kanji → meaning and readings, and its sentence → reading and meaning. The sentence card waits until you've learned the kanji card. Once you've seen all of a lesson's kanji, its story lines become cards too.

After you show the answer, grade yourself. Again brings the card back in a minute; Hard, Good and Easy push it further out each time you get it right, from a day to weeks and then months. New cards come in at the daily limit set below. It's all saved in this browser only, so use the backup box to move it to another device.

The kanji list is Jonathan Waller's (tanos.co.uk), the one behind Jisho and many Anki decks, plus nine common kanji it leaves out (分 無 可 的 身 阪 誰 俺 岡); they're marked +.
