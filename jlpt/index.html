Japanese, one lesson at a time.

Study pages for working towards the JLPT, kept apart from the lecture notes. Each one takes a single lesson and unpacks it: the key lines with furigana you can switch off, vocabulary with new example sentences, the grammar point, the casual forms around it, and a quiz. Tap ▶ on any Japanese line to hear it.
